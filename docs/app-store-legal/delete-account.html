Delete your Sara account

This page applies to Sara (ሳራ), listed on Google Play as Tigrinya Ai: Sara.

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in and open your profile with the top-right person or avatar button.
  2. Choose መለለዪ ሰርዝ (Delete account).
  3. Confirm deletion. If asked to sign in again, sign out, sign in, and repeat these steps.

Request deletion without the app

Email degenlogistics@gmail.com from the email address registered to your Sara account. Use the subject “Sara account deletion request” and state that you want your account and associated profile data deleted. Do not send your password. We verify ownership before processing a request; if you cannot access your registered email, contact support to discuss verification.

What is deleted and what remains

Deletion removes your Firebase Authentication account, including its email address, display name, and sign-in credentials. In-app deletion also clears the local profile information for that account.

Chats, attached photos, and your avatar are stored on your device rather than a synced chat account. A remote deletion request cannot erase device files: delete chats from history and remove your avatar in the app, or clear the app’s data on your device.

Account deletion does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel Sara Gold through Google Play subscriptions or Apple subscriptions. Store transaction records and subscription records handled by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat, and security or abuse-prevention records held by service providers, may remain under those providers’ retention requirements. Deletion does not remove records they are required to retain.
