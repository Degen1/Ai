መለለዪ

ዕላልን ስእልታትን

መልእኽቲ ወይ ስእሊ ክትሰድድ ከለኻ፡ መልሲ ንምድላው ናብ ኣገልጋሊ ሳራን OpenAIን ይለኣኽ። ዕላላትካ ንኻልኦት ተጠቀምቲ ኣይንሕትሞን። OpenAI ንድሕነት ኣገልግሎቱ መዝገብ ከዕቅብ ይኽእል።

ኣብ መሳርሒኻ ዝዕቀብ

ታሪኽ ዕላልካ፣ ናብ ዕላል ዝወሰኽካዮ ስእሊ፣ ስእሊ መገለጺኻን ምርጫ መልክዕካን ኣብ መሳርሒኻ ይዕቀቡ። ኣብ መንጎ መሳርሒታት ኣይተዛመዱን። ዕላል ካብ ታሪኽ ክትሰርዞ ትኽእል። መለለዪኻ ምስ ትሰርዝ፡ ኣብ መሳርሒኻ ዘሎ ታሪኽ ዕላል በይኑ ኣይስረዝን።

Sara Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Sara is a Tigrinya-first AI chat app. You can use chat without creating an account.

Accounts

If you create an account, Firebase Authentication processes your email address, password, display name, and technical information needed for authentication and security. You can delete your account in the app's profile settings.

Chats and photos

When you send a message or attach a photo, Sara sends it to its hosted chat service and OpenAI to generate a reply. Sara requests that OpenAI not store the response as application state. OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring logs under its API data controls. Sara does not publish your chats to other users.

Information saved on your device

Sara saves chat history, attached chat photos, your chosen profile picture, locally saved display name, and theme preference on your device. These items do not sync between devices. You can delete chats from history and remove your profile picture. Deleting your account clears its local profile information, but does not automatically erase separately stored chat history; delete those chats in history if you want to remove them.

Subscriptions

Sara Gold is optional. Apple processes App Store payments. RevenueCat processes purchase history and an app user identifier to check access and restore purchases. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple account settings.

Services and contact

Sara uses Expo EAS Hosting for its chat service, OpenAI for AI replies, Firebase Authentication for optional accounts, RevenueCat for subscription access, and Apple for App Store payments. The app also includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK, although no ads are currently displayed. The SDK may collect technical data such as IP address, device identifiers, diagnostics, and interaction data. These providers process information under their own privacy terms. We do not sell your chat content. For privacy questions or requests, email degenlogistics@gmail.com.
