Sara Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Sara is a Tigrinya-first AI chat app. You can use chat without creating an account.

Accounts

If you create an account, Firebase Authentication processes your email address, password, display name, and technical information needed for authentication and security. You can delete your account in the app's profile settings or follow the account deletion request instructions.

Chats and photos

When you send a message or attach a photo, Sara sends it to its hosted chat service and OpenAI to generate a reply. Sara requests that OpenAI not store the response as application state. OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring logs under its API data controls. Sara does not publish your chats to other users.

Information saved on your device

Sara saves chat history, attached chat photos, your chosen profile picture and locally saved display name on your device. These items do not sync between devices. You can delete chats from history and remove your profile picture. Deleting your account clears its local profile information, but does not automatically erase separately stored chat history; delete those chats in history if you want to remove them.

Response reports

If you choose to report an AI response, Sara sends the selected response (up to 24,000 characters), your selected reason, a random report identifier, and the app platform to a Firebase Cloud Function. The report is saved privately in Cloud Firestore for authorized Sara operators to review and improve content safety. Your full conversation, attached photos, account email, and profile picture are not included. Technical request information may be processed for security; an hourly hashed network-address bucket limits abuse. Reports are retained for review and safety follow-up. Contact support for privacy or deletion requests; security records may be retained where necessary.

Subscriptions

Sara Gold is optional. Apple processes App Store payments, and Google processes Google Play payments. RevenueCat processes purchase history and an app user identifier to check access and restore purchases. You can manage or cancel your subscription in the store account where you subscribed (Apple App Store or Google Play).

Services and contact

Sara uses Expo EAS Hosting for its chat service, OpenAI for AI replies, Firebase Authentication for optional accounts, RevenueCat for subscription access, Firebase Hosting for public support and policy pages, and Apple App Store or Google Play for store payments. Google AdMob provides banner and optional rewarded ads for free users. Completing a rewarded ad unlocks two more questions; Sara Gold has no ads and unlimited questions. We request non-personalized ads and do not send chat content to AdMob. Question credits are stored locally on this installation. Where required, you can revisit ad privacy choices from your profile. The SDK may collect technical data such as IP address, device identifiers, diagnostics, and interaction data. These providers process information under their own privacy terms. We do not sell your chat content. For privacy questions or requests, email degenlogistics@gmail.com.
